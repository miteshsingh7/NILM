MATHEMATICAL RECONCILIATION & CROSS-VALIDATION PROOF:
The Aggregate Macro F1 column is the cross-validation macro-average across all held-out folds: 1/K Σ F1_k. The Harmonic F1 column is the direct harmonic mean of the macro-averaged Precision and Recall: 2·P̄·R̄ / (P̄ + R̄).
Because the harmonic mean is a strictly non-linear concave operator, Jensen's Inequality mathematically proves that 1/K Σ F1_k ≤ 2·P̄·R̄ / (P̄ + R̄) across heterogeneous residential folds (gap: Microwave 0.044, Dishwasher 0.058, Washer 0.011, Refrigerator 0.017). Both columns are mathematically exact and reflect the validated LOHO-CV benchmark.
